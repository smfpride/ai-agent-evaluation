You must evaluate whether the actual response contains the correct product IDs (msind_productid) as specified in the expected response. Consider the following aspects:

1. **Product ID Match**: Do the msind_productid values in the actual response match those in the expected response?
2. **Complete Coverage**: Are all expected product IDs present in the actual response?
3. **Precision**: Does the actual response avoid including incorrect or unexpected product IDs?
4. **Ordering Relevance**: Are the most relevant product IDs prioritized appropriately?
5. **JSON Parsing**: Can the product IDs be successfully extracted from the **Products:** section JSON array?

## Scoring Guidelines

Provide a score from 0 to 1 based on the following scale:

- **0-0.2**: Highly inaccurate - None or very few of the expected product IDs are present

- **0.7-0.8**: Accurate - Most or all expected product IDs present with few incorrect additions
- **0.9-1.0**: Highly accurate - All expected product IDs present with minimal or no incorrect additions
