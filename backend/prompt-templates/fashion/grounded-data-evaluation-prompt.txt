## Evaluation Criteria

You must evaluate whether the actual response demonstrates that products are grounded in a real product catalog rather than being hallucinated or fabricated. Consider the following aspects:

1. **Product Catalog References**: Do the returned products include specific identifiers (msind_productid) suggesting they come from a real catalog?
2. **Structured Product Data**: Are products presented with consistent, structured attributes (IDs, names, descriptions, etc.)?
3. **Grounding Indicators**: Does the response show evidence of retrieval from a knowledge base rather than generation?
4. **Product Consistency**: Are the product details internally consistent and realistic for actual catalog items?
5. **Catalog Coverage**: Do the products appear to be legitimate items that would exist in a fashion product catalog?

## Scoring Guidelines

Provide a score from 0 to 1 based on the following scale:

- **0-0.2**: Not grounded - Products appear completely fabricated or hallucinated with no catalog references
- **0.3-0.4**: Minimally grounded - Weak evidence of catalog grounding; may be partially fabricated
- **0.5-0.6**: Moderately grounded - Some evidence of catalog grounding but missing key identifiers or inconsistencies present
- **0.7-0.8**: Well grounded - Clear evidence products come from catalog with proper IDs and structure
- **0.9-1.0**: Fully grounded - All products demonstrably from catalog with complete structured data and identifiers
